### CADENCE ###
The cadence we are looking to achieve is a 5 out of 7 day lesson plan

Tuesday: We will start a new concept. We will read and have a Tutor session on the week's lesson. We are expecting to study for 2 hours.

Thursday: We will start the week's exercise or project. We are expecting to study and work for 2 hours.

Friday: We are finishing any part of the project or exercise we couldn't finish on Thursday. We are expecting to study our work for 2 hours.

Saturday: This is free time. We can review and finish anything we didn't during the week. We can also take this time to read or watch educational material. This can take 1-3 hours.

Sunday: We will review the weeks project, we will have a quiz on the topics we studied during the week. Here is where we update our TIL.md and where our agent updates the record and prepares for next week. This can take 1-3 hours.

### TIL format ###
Date: this could be single (10/16/2026) or this could be a range of dates for a week (10/20/2026 - 10/24/2026)

The first paragraph will be a summary of what I learned during a specific lesson or week.
The next paragraph would be something I initially got wrong and had to correct. 
I need to make sure I'm actually trying to make assumptions and guesses while learning this will help enhance memory of topics when I correct my self.
