### CADENCE ###
The cadence we are looking to achieve is a 5 out of 7 day lesson plan

Tuesday: We will start a new concept. We will read and have a Tutor session on the week's lesson. We are expecting to study for 2 hours.

Thursday: We will start the week's exercise or project. We are expecting to study and work for 2 hours.

Friday: We are finishing any part of the project or exercise we couldn't finish on Thursday. We are expecting to study our work for 2 hours.

Saturday: This is free time. We can review and finish anything we didn't during the week. We can also take this time to read or watch educational material. This can take 1-3 hours.

Sunday: We will review the weeks project, we will have a quiz on the topics we studied during the week. Here is where we update our TIL.md and where our agent updates the record and prepares for next week. This can take 1-3 hours.

### TIL format ###
Date: this could be single (10/16/2026) or this could be a range of dates for a week (10/20/2026 - 10/24/2026)

The first paragraph will be a summary of what I learned during a specific lesson or week.
The next paragraph would be something I initially got wrong and had to correct. 
I need to make sure I'm actually trying to make assumptions and guesses while learning this will help enhance memory of topics when I correct myself.

### Review Ritual ###

Our reviews will occur in a variety of ways. Spaced repetition will probably be the most used system during this learning process. We are going to take strong spots and review them less often than our weak spots. Eventually, this will cause our weak spots to become stronger and our strengths will remain sharp. Our intervals will be 1 day -> 3 days -> 7 days -> 21 days. The due dates for these intervales will live on our learning record that is updated by our agent. The next technique we will utilize is Active recall. This is the "predict, then run" strategy. This is stronger because we will spend time thinking and making an educated prediction about how we think something will go. If we are wrong, the correction will stick in our memory better because we were already primed with a prediction. Finally we will try to explain concepts back in our own words as if we were teaching them. You know the saying "If you can't explain it simply, you don't know it well enough".

### Stuck Rule ###

The rule is simple. We don't ask for help for at least 25 minutes while we work on a problem. If we are stuck after that then we can ask for hints. We never ask for a full solution. From phase 0-2 we never have AI do the coding for us. We can stop for the day when our minimum expected study time is reached.
